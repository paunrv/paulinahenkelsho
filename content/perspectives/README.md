# Perspectives Editorial Guide

Perspectives are thoughtful essays grounded in real experience.

Not opinions written from a distance.

Not Product articles.

Not Field Notes.

## Purpose

Explore difficult questions that have no simple answer.

Readers are invited to think — not to be convinced.

## Four movements

1. Context
2. Tension
3. Reflection
4. Open Question

Do not end by giving the answer.

End with a question worth carrying.

## Reading experience

Continuous essay prose. Natural paragraphs. Sparse section breaks.

Do not break after every sentence. Whitespace only for scene, tone, idea, or emphasis.

## Structure

```markdown
# Title

Subtitle

---

Body in continuous paragraphs

---

Optional references

Closing question
```

## Final Rule

A Perspective should never tell readers what to think.

It should leave them unable to stop thinking.
