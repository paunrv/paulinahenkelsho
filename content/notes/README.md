# Notes — Product Architecture v2

Notes are the editorial heart of the website.

They are not different products.

They are different ways of thinking — one notebook.

## Philosophy

Projects demonstrate what Paulina builds.

Notes demonstrate how Paulina thinks.

The website exists to preserve both.

## Categories

Categories organize the shelf. They are content architecture, not site architecture.

No category should feel more important than another.

### Building

How products are built.

Product Discovery. UX. AI. Systems. Decision making. Real projects.

### Field Notes

Observations from real life.

Hospitals. Open water. Travel. Taekwondo. Wine country. Industrial visits. People. Moments.

### Perspectives

Long-form reflections born from lived experience.

Technology. Environmental responsibility. Industry. Latin America. Education. Ethics. Complex systems.

Not opinion pieces.

Thoughtful reflections grounded in real experience.

## Navigation

Top-level:

Home · Notes · About · CV

Perspectives is never a top-level section.

Visitors should feel they are browsing the same notebook.

## Language

Publish each note in the language in which it was born.

Never translate emotions, memories, or observations.

Spanish stays Spanish.

English stays English.

Optional frontmatter: `language: en | es`

## Reading Experience

Notes are designed for slow reading.

Reading takes priority over visual rhythm.

Group related sentences into natural paragraphs.

Separate only when:

- the scene changes
- the emotional tone changes
- the idea changes
- an important sentence deserves silence

Avoid breaking after every sentence.

Optimize for immersion.

The reading experience should feel closer to a magazine or a printed essay than a landing page.

## Visual Hierarchy

Category labels are subtle and equal:

BUILDING · FIELD NOTE · PERSPECTIVE

Same typography. Same size. Same weight.

No category dominates another.

## Frontmatter

```yaml
title:
subtitle:
category: Building | Field Notes | Perspectives
date: YYYY-MM-DD
readingTime:
featured: true|false
published: true|false
language: en | es
tags: []
```

Set `published: false` for drafts.

## Editorial Principle

Every publication belongs to Notes.

Some happen while building.

Some happen while observing.

Some happen while reflecting.

All of them belong to the same notebook.

That unity is more important than the category itself.

## Closing

Never optimize for SEO, engagement, or algorithms.

Optimize for truth, clarity, and permanence.

Quiet. Minimal. Observational. Honest. Literary.

Trust the reader.

Never explain what the reader already understands.

Never end with a moral.

Never force a lesson.

No CTA. No comments. No sharing buttons. No reactions.
